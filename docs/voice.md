# Life OS voice

How the app talks. Every string in `src/i18n/en.json` follows this.

## Sound like a friend, not a manual
- Short and warm. One idea per sentence. Most lines under 10 words.
- Everyday words. Say "habit", "week", "check-in", "journey". Never Sprint, Patch, Release, Backlog or Roadmap.
- Contractions are fine: you're, it's, don't, let's.
- Talk to "you". Speak about the app as "we" only when it helps ("We'll time your habits around it").

## Kind, never pushy
- No guilt. Missing a day is normal: "Happens to everyone", "You owe nothing".
- Celebrate small wins: "Nice one.", "All done. See you tomorrow!"
- No shouting, no exclamation marks in a row, no ALL CAPS sentences.

## Clear about numbers
- Use the habit's own unit: 500 ml, 7,000 steps, until 13:00. Minutes only for timed things.
- Say where a number comes from in plain words: "This week: 3 of 4 days".
- Science stays honest: "A pattern from your days, not proof."

## Buttons
- Say what happens: "Start my first week", "Yes, rest today", "Go tiny".
- Cancel buttons can be casual: "Never mind", "Nah, keep it".

## Examples
| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Fewer patches stick better. | Start small. Three is plenty. |
| Start Sprint 2 | Start week 2 |
| Review Sprint 03 | Do your weekly check-in |
| Streak safe. Nothing owed. | Your streak is safe. You owe nothing. |
